Our Story — Since 2008

Crafting Champions
for Over 15 Years

Founded in 2008 in the heart of Gunjianwali Kallan, Sialkot — the world's sports goods capital — Imperial Enterprises began as a small family workshop with a big dream: to craft sports goods that athletes across the globe could trust.

Today, we proudly manufacture, export, and supply premium sports equipment to over 60 countries, continuing our founders' legacy of quality, honesty, and craftsmanship.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Excellence

From a small workshop in Sialkot to a global sports goods manufacturer — every milestone shaped who we are today.

2008
The Beginning
Imperial Enterprises was founded in Gunjianwali Kallan, Sialkot — a small workshop with a vision to craft world-class sports goods.
2012
First Export Order
Our first international shipment to Europe opened the doors to global markets.
2015
ISO 9001 Certified
We achieved ISO 9001 certification, marking our commitment to international quality standards.
2018
Factory Expansion
Our manufacturing facility was expanded to serve growing global demand with modern machinery.
2022
60+ Countries
Our products reached over 60 countries across 5 continents, serving athletes worldwide.
Today
A Trusted Global Brand
We continue to innovate, craft, and deliver premium sports goods — carrying forward the legacy of our founders.
